Scripture quotations are from the King James Version (KJV), which is in the public domain.

The "In plain English" notes are short summaries written to reflect mainstream, long-standing Christian interpretation and the verse's original context, rather than any one denomination's distinctive views. They are a starting point for reflection, not a full commentary. A pastor or trusted teacher can help you go deeper.

This is a small reflection tool, not a substitute for a pastor, counselor, or doctor. If you are in crisis or thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (U.S. Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or contact your local emergency number.
